# Wordplay

**Play - Feel - Live.** Wordplay is a Scripture Engagement game. Players read, listen, watch and play their way through the Bible, and every game ends with **Head - Heart - Hands**: understand the passage, reflect on it, then act on it.

Wordplay runs in any modern web browser. Once it is installed, it opens and works offline, on Android, iPhone or a computer.

## The games

Wordplay has **ten games**. Most have more than one way to play, called a **format**. Each format is marked by its media type: **Text**, **Audio** or **Video**.

| # | Game | What you do | Formats | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Echo** | Read a passage, hide the text, then retell the story in your own words. Your retelling is checked for the key parts of the story. | Read and retell (Text); Listen, then retell (Audio) | Text playable. Audio coming soon. |
| 2 | **Scene Detective** | Watch a scene from a Gospel, Acts or Covenant film, or read it, then answer three questions about what happened, who was there and what was said. | Watch the film clip (Video); Read the scene (Text) | Text playable. Video coming soon. |
| 3 | **Covenant Quest** | Journey through the **12 clips of *The Covenant* film**, from creation to the Promised Land. Each clip has a question, a Heart moment and a Hands mission, and the next clip unlocks after you finish. | Video journey (Video); Read the journey (Text) | Text playable. Video coming soon. No book is chosen: the film sets the order. |
| 4 | **Who Said It** | Identify who spoke each Scripture quotation. | Listen, then choose (Audio); Type the speaker (Text); Choose the speaker (Text) | Text playable. Audio coming soon. |
| 5 | **Story Builder** | Put the events of a Bible story in order. The example is The Good Samaritan. | Type the order (Text); Arrange the cards (Text) | Playable. |
| 6 | **Memory Verse** | Learn a verse by heart: listen, fill the blanks, or type the whole verse from memory. | Listen, then type (Audio); Type from memory (Text); Fill the blanks (Text) | Text playable. Audio coming soon. |
| 7 | **Bible Trivia** | Ten random questions on Bible facts, with three lives. No question repeats within a quiz. | Type the answer (Text); Multiple choice (Text, with 50:50 and Hint lifelines) | Playable. |
| 8 | **Bible Characters** | Read the clues to a Bible character, one at a time, then guess. The earlier you guess, the more points: 5 after one clue, down to 1. | Type the name (Text); Choose the name (Text) | Playable. |
| 9 | **Bible Crossword** | Solve a crossword of Bible people and places from the clues. | Crossword grid (Text); Type the clues (Text) | Playable. |
| 10 | **Word Search** | Find Bible words hidden in a letter grid, by tapping the first and last letters. Words run across, down, diagonally, or backwards. | Find in the grid (Text); Unscramble (Text) | Playable. |

Covenant Quest is the only game without a book choice. All other games ask you to choose the **Old or New Testament**, then a **book**.

## Finding and playing a game

The **home page** shows the games in a **2 x 2 grid**, and each card lists its formats. Use the filters to show:

- **All:** every game.
- **Text:** every game that has a text format (all ten).
- **Audio:** games with an audio format (Echo, Who Said It, Memory Verse).
- **Video:** games with a video format (Scene Detective, Covenant Quest).

Tap a game to open its **game page**. It explains the game, lists how to play, and shows its formats. Choose a format, then tap **Start**.

**Steps:**
1. Open Wordplay and enter your name. Choose an avatar (twelve Bible characters) and your interface language.
2. Pick a game from the home page, using a filter if you want.
3. Read the game page and choose a format.
4. Choose a Testament and a book (not needed for Covenant Quest).
5. Play. Follow the instructions on screen.
6. At the end, write a short **Heart** reflection (private to you and your group or mentor), and choose a **Hands** mission for the week.
7. Review the **Answers** list to see each question with the correct answer.

## Difficulty levels

Choose **Easy**, **Medium** or **Hard** on the home page. The level applies to every game.

| Setting | Easy | Medium | Hard |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lives (Bible Trivia, Bible Characters) | 5 | 3 | 1 |
| Options in multiple choice (Bible Trivia, Who Said It) | 3 | 4 | 4 |
| Lifelines 50:50 and Hint (Bible Trivia) | Yes | Yes | No |
| Word count in Bible Crossword and Word Search | 6 | 8 | 10 |
| Blanks in Memory Verse | 2 | All | All |
| Echo key points shown before retelling | Yes | No | No |
| Head points multiplier | x1 | x1.5 | x2 |

The level is saved with each game in your history. Hard is for groups who know Scripture well. Easy suits beginners and children.

## Scoring, progress and rewards

Every completed game earns points:

| Part | Points |
|---|---|
| **Attendance** (completing a game) | 5 |
| **Head** (your score in the game, such as correct answers or words found), multiplied by your level (x1 Easy, x1.5 Medium, x2 Hard) | varies |
| **Heart** (finishing the reflection; the content is never scored) | 2 |
| **Hands** (completing the mission) | 3 |

- **Levels:** Seedling, Sprout, Disciple, Steward, Witness, Champion. Your level and the points to the next level appear on the home page.
- **Badges:** First Steps, Book Explorer, Old Testament Scholar, New Testament Scholar, Faithful Three (three days in a row), Heart Keeper, Hands in Action, and Full Set (try every game).
- **Day streak:** the number of days in a row you have played.
- **My progress:** your points, games, books, modules, badges, and a history of every game you attended (date, game, book and points). You can clear your history.
- **Leaderboard:** ranks the players on this device by points.
- **Answers review:** at the end of every game, each question is listed with a tick or cross and the correct answer.

Several players can use one device. Use **Switch player** or **Add a new player** on the profile screen. **Log out** returns to the sign-in screen, and your progress is kept.

## Bible text

Wordplay uses two kinds of Bible text.

**1. King James Version (built in).** The complete King James Version is part of the app, so Echo always has an English passage, even offline and without a key. This is the default.

**2. Bible versions from Bible Brain (optional).** On the home page, under **Add a Bible from Bible Brain**, you can add other Bibles:

1. Type your own **free Bible Brain key**. You can request one at https://4.dbt.io/api_key/request. The key is used only to fetch the text. It is kept in memory for the session, and it is **never saved**.
2. Choose a **country**. All countries in Bible Brain are listed.
3. Choose a **language** for that country.
4. Choose a Bible with text, and press **Download** for the Old Testament, New Testament or complete Bible.
5. When the download finishes, choose **Use this Bible**. The Bible is saved on your device and can be chosen again from the Bible dropdown.

**Where the chosen text is used:** Echo shows its passages in the Bible you chose. If the chosen Bible has no text for a book (for example, only the Old Testament is downloaded), Echo uses the built-in King James Version for that book. The other games use their built-in English text for now.

Check the licence of each Bible version before you use or share it. Bible Brain's terms apply to the text.

## Installing and offline use

Wordplay is a **Progressive Web App**. To install it:

- **Android (Chrome):** open the game, then choose **Install app** or **Add to Home screen** from the menu.
- **iPhone (Safari):** open the game, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Computer (Chrome or Edge):** choose the install icon in the address bar.

Once installed, the game screens and the built-in King James Version open **without an internet connection**. Downloaded Bibles also work offline. Audio and video need a connection, once they are available.

## Privacy and data

Wordplay has no accounts on a server and sends no analytics. Your progress stays on your device.

- **No accounts on a server.** Profiles, points, history and the leaderboard are stored on the device.
- **Bible Brain keys** are typed by the player for their own downloads. They are kept in memory, are not saved, and are sent only to Bible Brain.
- **Downloaded Bible text** is stored in the browser on the device.
- **No analytics** are sent by the game. The only outside service it contacts is **Bible Brain**, and only when a player uses the Bible chooser. Audio and video will contact the media server once they are set up.

To remove everything, clear the site's data in the browser settings, or uninstall the app.

## Content and accuracy

Wordplay is a learning tool. Its questions, clues, passages and summaries are written or generated from the Bible text, and they are being reviewed by partner teams. They may contain errors, and they are not an official study resource.

- Check any passage or answer against your own Bible before you use it with a group.
- Questions that were generated from the text test wording and reference, not deep knowledge.
- Theological accuracy of each item has not yet been signed off. Please report any error through the project's issue tracker.

## Known limits

- Only **Echo** uses the chosen Bible text. The other games use built-in English.
- **Audio and video** are not yet connected.
- **Game content** (questions, clues and summaries) is in English and needs translation and review by a Bible scholar and native speakers before wide use.
- **Question coverage by book (current):**

  | Game | Books with questions |
  |---|---|
  | Memory Verse | 66 |
  | Bible Trivia | 66 (3 John has nine questions; one comes from another book) |
  | Who Said It | 22 (generated from the text) |
  | Echo | 30 |
  | Bible Characters | 40 |
  | Bible Crossword | 45 |
  | Word Search | 45 |
  | Scene Detective | 4 |
  | Story Builder | 1 (Luke) |
  | Covenant Quest | Its 12 clips (no book choice) |

  Memory Verse, Bible Trivia and Who Said It questions are generated from the King James text, so they test wording and reference rather than deep knowledge. Echo, Bible Characters, Bible Crossword, Word Search, Scene Detective and Story Builder are written by hand, and cover only the books listed above. Bible Characters includes Torah, Judges, Samuel, Kings, Chronicles and Ezra-Nehemiah, and Bible Crossword and Word Search cover all the Gospels, Acts and the Pauline letters. More books are added in batches. Books without questions for a game are greyed out in its book picker. Bible Characters has one to three characters per book, so a round can be short.
- **Bible Brain downloads** take several minutes for a full Bible, because each chapter is requested separately.
- **Leaderboards** rank the players on one device only.
- **Sign-in** is choosing a player on the device. It is not password-protected.
- **Progress is saved on the device only.** Use **Export my progress** on the Progress screen to keep a copy. If the browser storage is full, the game tells you.
- **Bible Brain** must be used within its terms and the licence of each Bible.
